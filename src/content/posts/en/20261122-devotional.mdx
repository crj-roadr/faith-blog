# 1 Peter 4 Devotional: Blessed When Suffering for the Name of Christ

Today's devotional is found in the book of 1 Peter, chapter 4, and this is what happened.

**Synopsis:** In this chapter, Peter writes a letter addressed to Christians scattered throughout the world, commanding them to live according to the example of Christ, who suffered for our sins, enduring suffering for His name.

## Observations

1. **We are blessed to endure suffering and persecution for the name of Christ, because the Spirit of God rests upon us.** This is an observation that all of us need to hear as Christians. For in life, we will have many tribulations, but we must take heart because Jesus Christ overcame the world (John 16:33). Many times, we are so distressed and preoccupied with the problems we face that we end up losing the joy we have in Christ. Yet these very problems cause us to forget that we are persecuted and pass through tribulations because the Holy Spirit dwells within us. And just as Jesus Christ was persecuted for proclaiming the gospel and fulfilling God's Word, we too share in this suffering and persecution (John 15:20). The difference is that Christ suffered for our sins while being innocent, whereas we deserve suffering because we are sinners. Therefore, we should consider ourselves blessed, just as Jesus said, when we are targets of persecution for His name (Matthew 5:11). For this means that we have truly accepted Jesus Christ as our Lord and Savior. Consequently, we are heirs to God's inheritance and will have the privilege of living in His presence alongside Christ and all our brothers and sisters in Christ.
