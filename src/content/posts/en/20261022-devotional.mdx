# Devotional Psalm 119:9–16: How Can a Young Person Keep Their Way Pure?

Today's devotional is found in the book of Psalms, chapter 119, verses 9–16, and this is what happened.

**Synopsis:** In these verses, the psalmist asks how a young person can keep their way pure, and answers by treasuring God's Word in his heart. He prays that God would not let him stray from His commandments, and he declares his wholehearted pursuit of God. He rejoices in God's statutes, meditates on His precepts, and delights in His decrees so that he would not sin against Him.

## Observations

1. **We must pray for God's help to remain clean according to His will.** This is the most important observation we can make as Christians — especially if we believe that God is omnipotent, omniscient, and omnipresent (Revelation 1:8; 1 John 3:20; Psalm 139:7–10). God is the Alpha and the Omega, our Creator and the only witness who saw the creation of the universe (Genesis 1:1). He is the very standard of purity and holiness that Jesus commanded us to follow (Matthew 5:48). Therefore, no one is better placed to help us reach that goal than God Himself. This is why we must apprehend this truth through the fear of the Lord — that is, reverence, respect, and awe. For it is through that holy fear that we begin to gain wisdom and orient ourselves rightly in the world. Through obedience to God we begin to understand the reason behind His commands (Psalm 111:10). When we fear God and obey Him, we are much closer to remaining pure than when we attempt it in our own strength. Then comes faith and the acceptance of Christ as our Lord and Saviour. For God sent Him so that all who believe in Him should not perish but receive eternal life (John 3:16). When we accept Jesus Christ as Lord and Saviour, we receive the Holy Spirit of God (Ephesians 1:13). It is through the Holy Spirit dwelling within us that God enables us to walk according to His statutes — because without the Spirit we simply could not. I say this because of our sinful nature and our constant propensity to sin against God. Without the Spirit of God we become slaves to the desires of the flesh and cannot fulfil God's will, which is that we live to satisfy the desires of the Spirit (Galatians 5:16–17). This is the very reason the psalmist prays and pleads with God to help him, through His Spirit, to remain pure.
