# 2 Peter 3 Devotional: The Coming of Christ and the Patience of God

Today's devotional is found in the book of 2 Peter, chapter 3, and this is what happened.

**Synopsis:** In this chapter, Peter writes a letter addressed to Christians scattered throughout the world, exhorting them to be always ready with blameless conduct and eagerly awaiting, in the last days, the return of Jesus Christ.

## Observations

1. **We must always be ready with blameless conduct and eagerly awaiting the coming of Christ, because the day of the Lord will come like a thief.** This is the posture that all of us must adopt. We will not have second chances to place our faith in Christ by accepting Him as our Lord and Savior. God is never slow in fulfilling His promises. It is only we who mistakenly think He is delaying, but the reality is entirely the opposite. First, God is not bound by the dimension of time as we are. Because God created time, He stands outside of the timeline. That is why a thousand years to us is like a single day to God, and vice versa. Therefore, when we think God is slow in fulfilling His promise, He is in fact being extremely patient with us so that everyone may come to repentance and be saved while there is still time (2 Peter 3:8–9). We have only this earthly life to decide whether we surrender our lives into the hands of Christ or leave them in our own hands, for after death there are no further opportunities (Hebrews 9:27–28). Furthermore, we must never fall into the trap of presuming that we still have plenty of time, for life is fleeting—we are here today, and tomorrow we may be gone (James 4:14). Therefore, let us stop playing Russian roulette with our salvation and seek earnestly to know Jesus Christ, His commandments, His teachings, and His character, understanding that He is worthy of all our faith and hope. Let us surrender our hearts to Him as our Lord and Savior and receive the gift He has prepared for us—eternal life (John 3:16).
