# Devotional Titus 1: Qualifications of Leaders and False Teachers

Today's devotional is found in the book of Titus, chapter 1, and this is what happened.

**Synopsis:** In this chapter, Paul writes a letter to his son in the faith, Titus, instructing him on how to appoint leaders for the church in Crete. Paul explains that church leaders must be of blameless character, faithful to their spouse, and their children must be believers who are not open to the charge of debauchery or insubordination. Furthermore, Paul instructs him to sharply rebuke rebellious leaders, empty talkers, and deceivers who will surely emerge, whose main objective is dishonest gain by telling people whatever they want to hear.

## Observations

1. **We must choose carefully the religious leaders we follow.** This instruction given by Paul to Titus regarding how to appoint leaders in the church of Crete should inform us about what character our own spiritual leaders ought to possess. We must exercise great caution when choosing which church to attend and which spiritual leaders to follow. I say this because our walk of faith and spiritual health are profoundly affected by these choices; we can easily fall into error by attending the wrong church, following false spiritual leaders, or worshipping distorted images of God. Jesus warned us to take care not to become a stumbling block to others, yet many will inevitably do so for money or personal favor (Lucas 17:1–2). Furthermore, Jesus taught that in the last days false prophets will come disguised in sheep's clothing, but inwardly they are ravenous wolves. They may present themselves as genuine servants of God, but through their fruit and conduct we can unmask them. A tree is recognized by its fruit: good trees bear good fruit and bad trees bear bad fruit. A good tree cannot bear bad fruit, nor can a bad tree bear good fruit (Matthew 7:15–20). However, in order to identify and contrast the poor conduct of false leaders with the righteousness exemplified by Jesus Christ, we must know the Bible deeply. Without a deep knowledge of Scripture, it is exceedingly easy to be led astray by the false doctrines preached by deceptive teachers. Without God's standard of truth, we end up accepting what is corrupt simply because we do not know the authentic truth intimately. Therefore, reading the Bible to cultivate an intimate relationship with God, to walk in His way, to distinguish good from evil, to spot false prophets, and to defend our faith in Christ is absolutely essential. We must be diligent in our study of the Scriptures and seek God with all our heart so that we never fall into the traps of Satan and his followers.
