# Devotional 2 Timothy 3: The Signs of the Last Days and Faith

Today's devotional is found in the book of 2 Timothy, chapter 3, and this is what happened.

**Synopsis:** In this chapter, Paul writes in his second letter addressed to his son in the faith, Timothy, drawing his attention to the godlessness of the last days marked by people full of selfishness and greed; boastful, arrogant, abusive, disobedient to parents, ungrateful, unholy, callous, unforgiving, slanderous, without self-control, brutal, haters of good, treacherous, reckless, conceited, and lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of God.

## Observations

1. **Godlessness in the last days.** Paul warns Timothy that in the last days people will be lovers of themselves and lovers of money; boastful, proud, abusive, disobedient to parents, ungrateful, unholy, without self-control, haters of good, and lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of God (2 Timothy 3:1–4). These individuals will maintain an outward appearance of godliness, but their behavior will deny its power because they are false prophets. We know whether a tree is good or bad by its fruit: a good tree bears good fruit and a bad tree bears bad fruit. A good tree cannot bear bad fruit, nor can a bad tree bear good fruit (Matthew 7:15–20). Paul urges Timothy to turn away from such people, remember what he learned in the Scriptures, and stand firm in his faith (2 Timothy 3:10–11, 14–15). Although this warning was given by Paul to Timothy, it applies equally to us. One does not need to be a theologian or a scientist to see that these signs of the last days are all unfolding today. Licentiousness has become normalized through the sexual revolution, propaganda, entertainment, and the proliferation of social networks and dating apps. Children disrespect elders and especially their parents. Ostentatious displays of opulence and arrogance have become celebrated. Marriages and romantic relationships have largely turned toxic and transactional, as few are willing to be vulnerable, serve one another, and trust as they ought. In other words, people are guarded and bring far more selfishness than love to their relationships. Lastly, our culture has popularized cancel culture, falsely accusing neighbors and condemning them as guilty before any evidence is presented. These are only some of the signs, yet all of them and many more are clearly evident in our day. Therefore, we must heed Paul's counsel to Timothy and remain faithful servants of Christ.
