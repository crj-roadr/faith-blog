# Devotional Ephesians 3: The Mystery of Christ and Intercessory Prayer

Today's devotional is found in the book of Ephesians, chapter 3, and this is what took place.

**Synopsis:** In this chapter, Paul writes a letter addressed to the churches of Ephesus, explaining to them the mystery of Christ that from the beginning God had not revealed to everyone, but little by little to the prophets and later to the apostles. Furthermore, Paul takes the opportunity to pray to God for the Ephesians, asking that their hearts may be transformed by the Holy Spirit and that their faith in Christ may be strengthened.

## Observations

These were my observations.

1. **The mystery of Christ: God's gradual revelation.** God in His infinite love always had contingency plans in case we were irresponsible with our free will. Therefore, when God decided to create us in His image and likeness, this was a risk He was willing to accept (Genesis 1:26–27). After all, He is the Creator, almighty, omnipresent, and omniscient. That is to say, if anything bad were to happen through our fault, God already had, before creation, the solution to save us—just like a Father who does not want to see His children lost forever. God knew what would need to happen for us to be saved. However, He did not explicitly tell us all the details about how He would save us and when He would do so at the beginning for one reason—our faith. God needed to do things this way so that our faith would be the determining factor in our relationship with God and our salvation in Christ. For faith is, nothing more and nothing less, than evidence pointing in a direction. That is, faith manifests itself when we have clues to believe in something, but not concrete proof that removes all doubts, leading us to place our hopes and trust in the direction the clues point. In other words, if God told us exactly how everything was going to happen, we would not need to have faith in God and surrender our hopes to Him, because we would know definitively how everything would unfold. Moreover, this knowledge would influence our willingness to believe in God of our own free will. For many would come to believe in God simply because they had certainty about how they would be saved, and not out of love and trust in Him. Thus, faith only manifests itself when there are doubts alongside trust in someone or something. In this way, God can see who truly believes in Him out of love and faith, and who is merely interested in salvation. For this reason, throughout history, God gradually revealed His great plan little by little to the prophets and later to the apostles, so that we would remain alert and watchful until the final day.
2. **The importance of intercessory prayer for our neighbour's faith in Christ.** In the same way that when we learn something new that can help others, we share it; we must do the same with prayer. First, so that this person can experience the power of God and the blessings He brings when we pray. This leads them to reflect on the blessings they are receiving because of God. And, consequently, it helps draw that person closer and closer to God. Second, prayer is the tool we have that allows us to communicate directly with God and ask Him for help. For Jesus Christ Himself said that in the world we will have trouble, but to take heart because He has overcome the world (John 16:33). If we are going to face troubles and we have a Saviour who promised to save and help us, we must share this good news with our neighbours and pray that God strengthens their faith in Christ. For certainly, this person will face trials and will need a great friend (Jesus Christ) who will help them overcome these situations.
