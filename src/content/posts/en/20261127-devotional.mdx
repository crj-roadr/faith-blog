# 1 John 1 Devotional: Walking in the Light and Cleansing from Sin

Today's devotional is found in the book of 1 John, chapter 1, and this is what happened.

**Synopsis:** In this chapter, John writes a letter addressed to Christians scattered throughout the world, exhorting us to walk in the light because God is light and in Him there is no darkness at all.

## Observations

1. **We must walk in the light, as God is in the light, so that we have fellowship with one another, and the blood of His Son Jesus Christ cleanses us from all sin.** We cannot claim that we believe in God and yet walk in darkness, because our actions would contradict what we profess to believe. And when our actions do not match our words, there are only two possibilities. The first is that we do not yet realize that we are living in conflict with our beliefs—that is, we are being inconsistent. This can happen when we are infants in the faith and do not yet understand the Word well. But eventually, as we grow in knowledge and wisdom through the Word, we become aware of it and change our conduct. The second possibility happens when we already know that we are not living according to God's will, and yet we still do not change our ways. In other words, we are deliberately being hypocritical. This is what we must never do, for this is the very reason why unbelievers and new believers blaspheme the name of God (Romans 2:23–24). Furthermore, when we are hypocrites, we become a stumbling block to those younger in the faith, and that is one of the gravest things we could ever do (Luke 17:1–2). Therefore, whatever the reason may be for professing faith in God while acting to the contrary, we must change our attitude and behavior immediately. For our salvation depends on genuine faith in Jesus Christ as our Lord and Savior (John 3:16).
