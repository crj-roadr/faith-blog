# Devotional Psalm 119:1–8: The Joy of Walking in the Law of God

Today's devotional is found in the book of Psalms, chapter 119, verses 1–8, and this is what happened.

**Synopsis:** In this passage, the psalmist pleads with God not to forsake him, because he longs to establish his ways by keeping His statutes and becoming blessed in God's eyes.

## Observations

1. **Blessed are those who obey all of God's commandments and seek Him with all their heart.** Many times, we ask ourselves: why don't we have the same opportunities or ease as others? Why does everything in our life seem so hard while others have an easy life? First, no life is easy. There is no life easier than yours, because we all deal with problems, even if they are different. That is, one person may have a great social life but be financially broken; another may be financially well-off but have lost their family and friends; and another may have a great social life and be well-off financially, but suffer from severe health issues. In other words, we are all dealing with tribulations in our lives and we will always have them, just as Jesus prophesied (John 16:33). The difference is that the quality of our life is determined by our attitude toward problems. It is not by chance that Jesus told us to take heart because He has overcome the world. This statement from Jesus calms our anxieties and brings peace to our souls because it changes the way we deal with hardship. For believers in Christ, this promise allows us not to lose heart under circumstances because we know that our Lord and Savior keeps His promises. Second, this sense of having a life filled with blessings is the fruit of God's favor. That is, the person who is a faithful servant of God, who obeys His commandments and seeks Him with all their heart, ends up receiving more blessings in this life and in the one to come (Psalm 119:1–2, Mark 10:29–30). For the faithful servant of God will have spiritual peace, joy, healthy relationships with others, good habits, and all the other qualities that open doors to living a life overflowing with blessings. Because whoever follows God's ways is learning and being transformed by the wisest and most powerful Being in the universe. In other words, God is the best psychologist, doctor, financial counselor, motivator, educator, and guide in the universe. Therefore, we should not be surprised when such a person lives a full and complete life, because they have been transformed by God. Above all, Jesus Christ is the way, the truth, and the life (John 14:6). There is no better path to walk, no greater truth to understand, and no better life to live. That is why, if we want to live a life full of blessings, our feet must be firmly grounded on solid rock. We must be anchored in the truth and in the way. For it is only when we fear, know, and obey God that we begin to acquire the wisdom, intelligence, and understanding necessary to lead a life full of joy and blessings (Proverbs 1:7, 9:10, and Psalm 111:10).
