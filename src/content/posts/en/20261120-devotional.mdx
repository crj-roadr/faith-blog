# 1 Peter 2 Devotional: Submission to Authority and Christ's Love

Today's devotional is found in the book of 1 Peter, chapter 2, and this is what happened.

**Synopsis:** In this chapter, Peter writes a letter addressed to Christians scattered throughout the world, commanding them to submit to every human authority for the Lord's sake.

## Observations

1. **We must submit to rulers and masters, both good and bad, for it is God's will that by doing good we silence the ignorance of foolish people.** This is an observation that sounds difficult to understand, and many do not like it. I understand why. According to worldly wisdom, we should not treat with love and kindness superiors who mistreat us, let alone submit to them. Being subjected to difficult and humiliating situations like these does not sit well with us. This situation is even worse when it comes to rulers we elected to fulfill their promised agenda, yet they end up subjecting people to poverty, crime, and censorship. Our natural reaction toward such rulers is indignation, anger, and rebellion. But Jesus Christ came to change our entire way of seeing, perceiving, and interpreting the world. Jesus brought a completely radical perspective that goes against common sense. First, He answered that God's greatest commandment is to love God with all our heart, soul, mind, and strength, and the second is to love our neighbor as ourselves (Matthew 22:36–40). Second, Jesus also gave us His commandment, which is to love one another as He loved us (John 13:34). Finally, Christ also commanded us to be perfect, just as our Father in heaven is perfect (Matthew 5:48). With all this context, we can now better understand why we must submit to our masters and rulers, even when they oppress us. The reason is that Jesus commanded us, as His followers, to love our neighbor just as He loved us. That is, to love them as Christ loved us even when He was mistreated, slandered, scourged, humiliated, and innocently crucified. For it is only by following the example Jesus left us that we demonstrate our true love for Him as His faithful followers and honor His sacrifice for us (John 14:15).
