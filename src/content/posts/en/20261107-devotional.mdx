# Devotional Hebrews 6: The Certainty and Security of God's Promises

Today's devotional is found in the book of Hebrews, chapter 6, and this is what happened.

**Synopsis:** In this chapter, the author writes a letter addressed to Christians of Jewish background, reminding them of the absolute certainty of the promise that God made to Abraham.

## Observations

1. **We can be confident and anchor all our hopes in the promise God made to Abraham, confirmed by an oath sworn by Himself.** This is the truth that brings us the greatest comfort and fills us with enduring hope as Christians. We know that our faith is anchored in the safest possible harbor. For God may seem slow from our human perspective, but He never fails and never leaves a single promise unfulfilled. This is the supreme distinction between God and everything else in the universe. We all know that we cannot place our ultimate hope in human beings, because they will inevitably let us down in one way or another. This is unavoidable, as we are finite, frail, and mortal creatures. Due to our limitations, human promises cannot be fully relied upon over the long term. With God, however, the very opposite is true. God is the Creator of the universe. Therefore, He transcends the universe and is omnipotent, having brought it into being out of nothing (Genesis 1:1). God created time, and for that reason He exists outside the timeline (He is eternal, having always existed and enduring forever). God is omniscient, knowing everything that takes place throughout His creation. Moreover, God is omnipresent, being present everywhere in the universe simultaneously (He is infinite and cannot be confined to any space). Because of His divine nature, there is no greater or more trustworthy foundation upon which to place our faith. Trusting Him and surrendering our lives to Him is the wisest decision we can ever make. As if that were not enough, God is perfect, loves us unconditionally, and desires our highest good. Thus, when God promised Abraham that He would bring forth from him a great nation and that through his offspring all nations on earth would be blessed, we are certain that He will accomplish it (Genesis 12:2–3; 18:18; 22:18). God not only made the promise to Abraham, but He also bound Himself by an oath, swearing by His own name to fulfill it. Furthermore, this promised Seed of Abraham who would bring blessing to all nations was fulfilled in Jesus Christ, who came to redeem us from death and hell. That is why we must believe in Jesus Christ as our Lord and Savior, receiving the gift of salvation that God freely gives by grace through faith (Ephesians 2:8–9).
