# Devotional Ephesians 5: Marital Duties and Marriage in Christ

Today's devotional is found in the book of Ephesians, chapter 5, and this is what took place.

**Synopsis:** In this chapter, Paul writes a letter addressed to the churches of Ephesus, explaining to them the marital duties between men and women. Furthermore, Paul continues by instructing them on conduct worthy of a Christian and warns them against disobedience to God.

## Observations

These were my observations.

1. **Marital duties and mutual submission.** Paul explains to the couples in Ephesus what the marital duties are according to the will of God. He begins by asking wives to submit to their husbands in everything, just as the church submits to Christ. Furthermore, Paul calls on wives to respect their husbands in all circumstances. However, he also asks husbands to love their wives just as Christ loved the church—even to the point of being willing to die for her. In addition, he adds that a husband must love his wife just as he loves himself in all cases (Ephesians 5:21–33). In other words, what Paul is asking of spouses is that both submit to one another out of reverence for Christ, but in distinct ways. He uses the analogy of the body of Christ to illustrate the relationship between the two sexes in marriage. For the body of Jesus representing the church is the wife, and the head representing Jesus is the husband. That is to say, the husband is the head of the wife and carries out the same role in marriage that Christ does in the church. And the wife is the body of the husband, carrying out the same role in marriage that the church does in Christ. This presupposes that both husband and wife are faithful followers of Christ and that both exhibit conduct worthy of a Christian. Such a man possesses the fruits of the Spirit and certain gifts as well—specifically, the gift of leadership following the example of Jesus. For according to Jesus, a leader serves others and does not come merely to command and be served (Matthew 20:28). A leader according to Jesus leads by setting the example of righteous conduct so that those who follow have no other choice than to follow suit. That is why Jesus took the initiative to wash the feet of His apostles and told them that a servant is never greater than his master (John 13:15). In other words, if Jesus Himself, who was the master of the apostles, served them, none of them could be arrogant and refuse to serve one another. It is this example of Christ that the husband, out of reverence for Him, must follow, and to which the wife should submit in all situations. Furthermore, a man who loves his wife as himself, seeing that she is his own body, will lay down his life for her, just as Jesus laid down His life for us (the body of Christ). Therefore, this is the context of mutual submission between spouses that Paul commends: the wife must submit to and respect her husband in all situations; and the husband must love his wife as himself and be willing to sacrifice his life for her.
