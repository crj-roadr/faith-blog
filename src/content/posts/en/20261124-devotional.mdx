# 2 Peter 1 Devotional: The Truthfulness of Scripture and Witnesses of Christ

Today's devotional is found in the book of 2 Peter, chapter 1, and this is what happened.

**Synopsis:** In this chapter, Peter writes a letter addressed to Christians scattered throughout the world, reminding them of the truthfulness of the Scriptures.

## Observations

1. **Jesus Christ exists, is the Son of God, and the apostles, together with others, witnessed His honor and glory.** This is the best news for everyone (especially Christians) and the great distinction between Christianity and other religions. For Christians, our faith is not blind. That is to say, our faith is validated by the reality of Jesus Christ as a historical person who entered our world, lived a perfect life, taught the deepest knowledge of God, died for our sins, and rose again to prove to us that He is the Christ, our Lord and Savior. In other words, our faith is grounded on the evidence of numerous eyewitnesses and not merely on groundless beliefs. Therefore, our faith is verifiable and can be examined and confirmed. That is why Paul stated that if Jesus Christ was not raised as He promised, then our faith is in vain, because it relies completely on the credibility of Christ (1 Corinthians 15:14–17). For He prophesied to His disciples that He would die and rise on the third day (Matthew 16:21; Mark 8:31; and Luke 9:22). That is why placing our hope in Jesus Christ is the wisest decision anyone can ever make. For we are trusting in the only human being who came into our world, declared that He would die and rise on the third day, and actually did it.
