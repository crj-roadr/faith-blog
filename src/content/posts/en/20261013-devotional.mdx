# Devotional 1 Thessalonians 1: A Testimony of Faith that Inspires

Today's devotional is found in the book of 1 Thessalonians, chapter 1, and this is what happened.

**Synopsis:** In this chapter, Paul writes his letter, addressed to all believers in Christ Jesus who are in Thessalonica, commending them and giving thanks to God in his prayers for the faith and conviction in Christ Jesus that they demonstrate. He feels proud knowing that the faith of the Thessalonians is so great that it is influencing everyone across the region (Macedonia and Achaia). Many are turning to Christ because of their ministry and example.

## Observations

1. **We must pray and give thanks to God for all Christians whose testimony of faith is influencing others to draw closer to Christ.** This observation is of utmost importance. Following the example of Christ is the greatest challenge we all face due to the fact that He is a perfect human being (1 Peter 2:22). And our tendency to sin turns this challenge into an impossible mission. Not to mention that many of us (myself included), for much of our lives, did not live by the Spirit and did not proclaim the gospel as Christ commanded us (Galatians 5:16–17, Mark 16:15). For these reasons, we must give thanks to God when there are people faithfully following the example of Jesus, and pray in intercession for them. Because believers who are truly faithful to Christ in their conduct are a rarity nowadays. And seeing that we are in a spiritual warfare where the enemy seeks our total destruction, the least we can do is ask God to protect them from all tribulations and strengthen their faith. Because Christians like this are no longer of this world; they were chosen out of this world by Jesus and transformed in Him. Because of this, they will be rejected and persecuted in this world. For the world rejects everything that does not belong to it (John 15:18–19). Even Jesus Himself, who is perfect and not of this world, was persecuted and crucified. Therefore, the same will happen to all faithful followers of Christ, because a servant is never greater than his master (John 15:20). That is, they will have to endure waves of tribulations and afflictions. Because they are fighting head-on against the enemy and doing the work that most do not have the courage to do. For all these reasons, we must do our utmost to protect them through our prayers and support them in whatever they need.
