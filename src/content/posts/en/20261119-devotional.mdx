# Devotional 1 Peter 1: A Living Hope and the Call to Holiness

Today's devotional is found in the book of 1 Peter, chapter 1, and this is what happened.

**Synopsis:** In this chapter, Peter writes a letter addressed to the Christians scattered throughout the world, exhorting them to praise God for a living hope and to be holy just as Jesus Christ is holy.

## Observations

1. **We must praise God for a living hope, for He has caused us to be born again to an imperishable inheritance through the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead.** This is the greatest news for all humanity, for God fulfilled the promise He made to Abraham when He said that all nations would be blessed through his offspring (Genesis 12:1–3). Prior to Christ's coming, there was profound mystery surrounding how exactly all nations would be blessed through Christ, as well as the specific details of His messianic mission. Furthermore, we were captive under the yoke of the Law because we continually fell into sin (Romans 6:14). We could not redeem ourselves before God, because our animal sacrifices for the expiation of sins were insufficient to save us permanently (Psalm 143:2 and Titus 3:5). In other words, worshippers had to continually offer pure animals whenever they committed a sin (Leviticus 1:3–4; 4:27–28). That was the unbearable yoke we carried before Christ came. But once Christ fulfilled prophetic scripture through His perfect, once-for-all sacrifice, we were finally liberated from this heavy burden that no human being could bear. Therefore, we ought to give profound thanks to God for sending His only begotten Son so that whoever believes in Him should not perish but have eternal life (John 3:16).
2. **We must be holy just as Jesus Christ was holy.** This is the mindset and attitude we must all cultivate as Christians. The fact that we are justified by faith in Christ rather than by the works of the Law does not mean we can cast off God's moral law and live however we please. On the contrary, the true way we demonstrate genuine repentance, gratitude, and love for Christ is by treasuring and obeying His commandments. We cannot simply claim to be repentant, grateful, and in love with Jesus while living in ongoing defiance of everything He taught. Doing so proves that our faith is counterfeit rather than genuine—we would merely be masquerading as followers of Christ. That is why Jesus declared that whoever loves Him will keep His commandments (John 14:15, 21). Therefore, if we truly desire to honor God and be called disciples of Jesus Christ, we must emulate Him, striving daily to be holy just as Jesus is holy.
