# Devotional 2 Corinthians 6: Hardships and the Unequal Yoke

Today's devotional is found in the book of 2 Corinthians, chapter 6, and this was what happened.

**Synopsis:** In this chapter, Paul writes in his second letter to the church of Corinth, frankly explaining the manifold hardships and trials they as apostles endure for following Jesus Christ. Furthermore, he sternly warns believers against idolatry, urging them never to be unequally yoked with unbelievers.

## Observations

These were my observations.

1. **A faithful follower of Christ must be transparent about hardships so as not to become a stumbling block to others.** This is an essential truth that many believers overlook on their spiritual journey, often due to false expectations. Failing to recognize that we will encounter afflictions as Christians means failing to understand the true gospel of Christ. When we misunderstand the gospel, we resemble the seed sown among thorns in the parable of the sower (Matthew 13:22), embracing faith under the illusion that our earthly lives will immediately become smooth and effortless. Then, when difficulties intensify and problems arise, many stumble and abandon their faith because they never grasped two fundamental biblical truths. The first is that we inhabit a world broken and fallen in sin; when Adam and Eve sinned and were cast out of Eden, the earth was cursed (Genesis 3:17), bringing mortality, hardship, and pervasive brokenness into our daily existence. The second is that a servant is never greater than his master (John 15:20–21). Jesus Christ came proclaiming the gospel of salvation as a perfectly righteous, sinless man, yet He was relentlessly persecuted and crucified for speaking the truth. If our Lord suffered unjustly, we as imperfect sinners cannot presume that our walk will be free from trial. We must expect tribulations, yet face them with courage and good cheer because Jesus has conquered the world (John 16:33). We must convey this candid reality to new believers and seekers alike, so we never promote a deceitful prosperity gospel that causes others to stumble.
2. **A faithful follower of Christ must turn away from idolatry and never form an unequal yoke with an unbeliever.** This observation is equally vital. It echoes the very first commandment of God: "You shall have no other gods before Me" (Exodus 20:2–3, Leviticus 22:31). Guarding against idolatry is especially challenging in our modern era, where society continually exalts and worships worldly pursuits—power, fame, wealth, sexual indulgence, and celebrity. Paul cautioned the Corinthians because they lived in a culture that valued worldly passions above the things of God, and that warning speaks directly to us today. We must deliberately separate ourselves from thoughts, habits, and partnerships that foster spiritual compromise and idolatry (2 Corinthians 6:14–17). We cannot immerse our lives in worldly environments, adopting the ways of the ungodly, while claiming to follow Christ. The desires of the flesh and the desires of the Spirit stand in direct opposition to each other (Galatians 5:16–17); they cannot coexist in harmony. For where your treasure is, there your heart will be also (Matthew 6:21). Therefore, we must not bind ourselves in intimate alliances with those who reject the light and prefer darkness. If we linger closely in the company of worldly compromise, we will inevitably find ourselves adopting the very vices and empty pursuits we were called to leave behind.
