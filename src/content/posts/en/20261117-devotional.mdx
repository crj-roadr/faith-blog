# Devotional James 4: Why Friendship with the World Is Enmity with God

Today's devotional is found in the book of James, chapter 4, and this is what happened.

**Synopsis:** In this chapter, James writes a letter addressed to the Christians of the twelve tribes of Israel scattered throughout the world, explaining to them why we must submit to God if we want to be saved from the hell that awaits us.

## Observations

1. **Friendship with the world is enmity with God.** This observation made by James is one that all Christians need to realize. For what the world approves of is precisely what God condemns. Therefore, we must be careful and seek God's wisdom and knowledge so that we can distinguish good from evil (that is, what God recommends from what the world recommends). Only by discerning this can we recognize when we are disobeying and betraying God, and whether we are pursuing friendship with the world when we should be seeking friendship with God. For example, the world tells us that we should take advantage of our youth to live life (partying, fornicating, experimenting with drugs, etc.), because we will have plenty of time to take life seriously later on. However, God tells us exactly the opposite: He urges us to take our lives seriously and accept Jesus Christ as our Lord and Savior, because on account of our sins we are condemned to death and we do not know what tomorrow brings (John 3:16). The world teaches us to idolize people whom, according to our worldly criteria, we deem worthy of worship (the rich, powerful, attractive, etc.), but God tells us that we must never idolize any human being, for God alone is worthy of worship (Exodus 20:3–5). Finally, the world teaches us that if we want to have a good life, we must be shrewd, abandon integrity, and embrace corruption. However, Jesus tells us that if we want to have a good life, we must seek first the kingdom of God, and then all these things will be added to us (Matthew 6:33). Therefore, as we can see, disobeying God to follow the teachings of the world is seeking enmity with God. And as Christians who claim to love God, we must keep His commandments (John 14:23).
