# Devotional Hebrews 1: The Supremacy and Divinity of Jesus Christ

Today's devotional is found in the book of Hebrews, chapter 1, and this is what happened.

**Synopsis:** In this chapter, the author writes a letter addressed to Christians of Jewish background, explaining to them the supremacy of Jesus Christ as the heir of all things and through whom God made the universe. Furthermore, the author explains that Christ perfectly reflects the radiance of God's glory and is the exact representation of His being.

## Observations

1. **Jesus is the King.** This is the message we find between the lines and that John affirms in the book of Revelation, chapter 1, verses 5–6. For Jesus is the King of kings and the only one chosen by God to sit at His right hand until the day of final judgment. This is the Old Testament prophecy that was fulfilled in the New Testament (Psalm 110:1; Mark 16:19). It was also through Jesus that the entire universe was created, because before coming into the world as a human being, Jesus was the Word of God, that is, God the Son (John 1:1–3; Hebrews 1:2–3). That is to say, Jesus Christ is also God, and He sustains all things by His powerful word. That is why denying the divinity of Jesus Christ and simply viewing Him as a good prophet or teacher is mistaken. We would be denying Christ's true identity. Moreover, Jesus does not leave open the option of considering Him merely a good teacher. Jesus behaved as God by accepting worship, forgiving sins, referring to Himself with the divine name "I AM", and identifying Himself as one with God and as the Son of God (John 20:28; Matthew 9:2; John 8:58, 10:30, and 5:17–18). In other words, Jesus forced people to make a choice: accept Him as the God He is, or reject Him as a blasphemer claiming equality with God. Thus, we can never consider Him a good teacher if we do not believe He is God when He Himself claims to be God. Therefore, Jesus truly is who He said He is—both man and God. It is up to us to believe in Him as our Lord and Savior so that we may be saved from the death and hell that await us.
