# 1 Peter 5 Devotional: Leadership with Humility and Submission in the Church

Today's devotional is found in the book of 1 Peter, chapter 5, and this is what happened.

**Synopsis:** In this chapter, Peter writes a letter addressed to Christians scattered throughout the world, exhorting church leaders to shepherd the flock of God under their care wholeheartedly and calling younger people to submit to their leaders.

## Observations

1. **Church leaders must shepherd the flock of God under their care, setting an example with humility and without tyranny.** This instruction is one that all our church leaders should follow. But unfortunately, those who truly follow this instruction faithfully are in the minority. And that is why there are many people who abandon faith in God, or do not even try to know Him better, because of leaders who do not represent God's name well. That is why it was no coincidence when Paul rebuked hypocritical religious leaders—who preach one thing and do the exact opposite—for causing people to blaspheme the name of God (Romans 2:21–24). It is for reasons like these that Paul instructed Timothy on how religious leaders should be chosen and what qualifications they must have. Paul said that they must be men of blameless reputation, faithful to one wife, with Christian children who follow the Word, and not lovers of money (1 Timothy 3:1–13). In other words, our leaders should strive to the utmost to follow the example of conduct of Jesus Christ, who is our High Priest. Furthermore, these qualifications that religious leaders must meet serve as a lens through which we can distinguish good leaders from bad ones.
2. **Young people must be submissive to their leaders with humility in their mutual dealings.** And we, as young people, must submit to our religious leaders with humility, just as the apostles did with Jesus Christ. That is why we must choose the right religious leaders to follow. And the only way to choose the right leaders is by building a relationship with God, knowing His Word, asking Him for discernment, and understanding the biblical qualifications for a religious leader. Because only by living God's Word will we have the desire and the capacity to submit to their leadership.
