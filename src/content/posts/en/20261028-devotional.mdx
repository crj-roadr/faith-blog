# Devotional 2 Timothy 4: Preach the Word in Season and Out of Season

Today's devotional is found in the book of 2 Timothy, chapter 4, and this is what happened.

**Synopsis:** In this chapter, Paul concludes his second letter to his son in the faith, Timothy, giving him the solemn charge to preach the word in season and out of season without ceasing to teach. For the time will come when people will not endure sound doctrine, but having itching ears, will accumulate for themselves teachers to suit their own passions. They will turn away from listening to the truth and wander off into myths.

## Observations

1. **We must continue preaching the gospel of Christ even when it is out of season.** This message from Paul could not be more timeless. Everything he prophesied would happen has indeed come to pass. He told Timothy that a time would come when true and sound doctrine would neither be accepted nor tolerated, because people would be so far from God's way that they would not even be able to bear it. This happens because most people will be enslaved to the flesh. That is, when we begin feeding and gratifying the desires of the flesh, those carnal desires automatically become more intense and insatiable. These desires begin to choke out the desires of the Spirit. Consequently, we cease to desire what the Spirit desires and begin longing solely to satisfy fleshly cravings. In other words, we become slaves to the flesh, because the flesh and the Spirit desire what is contrary to each other. Therefore, they are opposites and cannot coexist. When we satisfy one, we begin desiring to satisfy it more and more at the expense of the other (Galatians 5:16–17). Precisely because society is enslaved to the pleasures of its own flesh, it cannot accept or tolerate sound doctrine or anything related to the Spirit of God (that is, everything God deems good). For this reason, people turn to false prophets and false teachers who tell them the soothing lies they want to hear (2 Timothy 4:3). The time Paul spoke of is already here, as all the signs he mentioned are unfolding in our society today. Christians are being persecuted in various nations for believing in Jesus Christ as Lord and Savior, just as Jesus foretold (John 15:20). Christians who preach sound doctrine are cancelled and heavily criticized. People are already being led by their fleshly desires to idolize false teachers and follow deceptive doctrines. In short, we are already living in the last days. Therefore, we must heed Paul's exhortation: continue persevering in the faith, pray without ceasing, keep God's commandments, and preach the true gospel of Christ.
