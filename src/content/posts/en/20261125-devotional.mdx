# 2 Peter 2 Devotional: Beware of False Teachers and Heresies

Today's devotional is found in the book of 2 Peter, chapter 2, and this is what happened.

**Synopsis:** In this chapter, Peter writes a letter addressed to Christians scattered throughout the world, warning them that just as there were false prophets among the people, so there will also be false teachers.

## Observations

1. **We must be very careful of false teachers who introduce destructive heresies, even to the point of denying the very Christ who bought them.** This is a very important observation for our times. We do not need to be experts in the law to realize that there are teachers who are literally distorting the law of God. I say this because these laws are not ambiguous; God clearly prohibits certain behaviors and expresses His displeasure with those who transgress them. For example, God explicitly stated that a man shall leave his father and mother and be joined to his wife, and the two shall become one flesh—that is, the two are united as one through sexual union (Genesis 2:24). This is the biblical design of marriage according to the Word of God, for in God's eyes there is no such thing as casual sex; from the moment a man and a woman engage in sexual relations, they are spiritually joined (that is, married) in the eyes of God. Furthermore, God explicitly forbids homosexual relations, declaring them an abomination (Leviticus 18:22, 20:13, and Romans 1:26–27). Therefore, all these passages make God's will crystal clear, leaving no room for alternative interpretations. Yet today there are religious leaders who attempt to modernize God's Word for our contemporary culture, asserting that God permits homosexual relations and same-sex marriage. This is clearly a heresy that directly opposes God's sound doctrine. This is just one example among many today of leaders and teachers of God's Word who twist sound doctrine to satisfy their own vices (such as human approval, popularity, greed, etc.). Therefore, as Christians, we must be thoroughly grounded in the Word of God so that we can identify teachers who are leading people toward destruction. Only in this way can we protect ourselves from persuasive and manipulative individuals who exploit the faith and ignorance of believers to satisfy fleshly desires that endanger our salvation and lead toward condemnation.
