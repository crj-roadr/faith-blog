# Devotional 2 Thessalonians 3: Prayer and the Dignity of Work

Today's devotional is found in the book of 2 Thessalonians, chapter 3, and this is what happened.

**Synopsis:** In this chapter of his second letter addressed to all believers in Christ Jesus who are in Thessalonica, Paul asks them to pray for the rapid spread and honorable reception of the gospel everywhere in the world. Furthermore, Paul exhorts them not to associate with anyone among them who lives in idleness and laziness, becoming a burden to others.

## Observations

1. **We must pray for the rapid spread and honorable reception of the gospel.** This was the exhortation Paul gave, and I share the very same sentiment. You do not need to live in the world for very long to notice that things are going poorly and tend to worsen. Many nations were already undergoing hardships and trials before the pandemic, and conditions have only deteriorated. Today, there are still people living below the poverty line, lacking basic necessities (such as clean drinking water, electricity, sanitation, food, healthcare, etc.) and facing severe difficulties finding employment. Moreover, under the current economic system (without the gold standard) where central banks can inject currency into circulation to stimulate the economy at their own discretion, inflation rates and currency devaluation continue to climb uncontrollably. And as if that were not enough, international tensions are escalating, and the number of wars that have erupted continues to rise. In other words, no matter where we look, we find overwhelming problems that need solutions. This corroborates Jesus' prophecy when He said that in this world we will have tribulations, but to take heart because He has overcome the world (John 16:33). Furthermore, the only true solution for humanity's problems is to walk by the Spirit. We find ourselves in this condition because we strayed from God and consequently became slaves to sin and the desires of the flesh. All because the desires of the flesh stand opposed to the desires of the Spirit (Galatians 5:16–17). It is only when every creature in the world receives the gospel that the world will change for the better. For it is the Holy Spirit who converts and transforms the human heart for the good (Ezekiel 36:26–27).
2. **We must work to earn our living as Christians and not live in idleness and laziness.** This was one of the commandments God gave to mankind in the Garden of Eden, establishing work as part of the human vocation from the beginning (Genesis 2:15). It was also the sentence God pronounced after Adam listened to Eve's voice and disobeyed God (Genesis 3:17, 19). Therefore, we must obey God and work not only to earn our living, but also because labor confers dignity. Not to mention that when a person works and provides for their own needs, they have something to share with those who are in need (Ephesians 4:28)—which is precisely what Jesus taught us to do (Acts 20:35).
