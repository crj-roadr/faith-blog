# Devotional Hebrews 5: The Danger of Apostasy and Spiritual Immaturity

Today's devotional is found in the book of Hebrews, chapter 5, and this is what happened.

**Synopsis:** In this chapter, the author writes a letter addressed to Christians of Jewish background, warning them against apostasy.

## Observations

1. **When we stop listening to God and following His ways, we regress spiritually from adulthood back to infancy.** This observation may sound strange, but it is deeply true. When we drift away from God and begin to live according to our own opinions, we listen less and less to the voice of the Holy Spirit. When this happens, our hearts harden and we become slaves to the desires of the flesh. The trouble is that the flesh desires what is contrary to the Spirit. That is why the desires of the Spirit and the desires of the flesh cannot peacefully coexist. When we start indulging the one, we end up despising the other. In other words, we desire to satisfy the cravings of the flesh more, and we rarely deny them (Galatians 5:16–17). This is the grave danger of turning aside from God's path. Furthermore, it is in this exact sense that the author speaks when warning that we become like infants again when we continually disobey God. For we cease to heed the voice of His Spirit, and this causes a severe regression in our spiritual growth. The fruit of the Spirit that was budding in us rots away instead of ripening. The spiritual gifts that we received from the Holy Spirit atrophy instead of flourishing (Romans 12:6–8; 1 Corinthians 12:8–10). That is, the further we regress spiritually, the more immature we become, behaving like spiritual toddlers. Therefore, if we desire to continue progressing spiritually in our walk with God, we must obey Him and drink deeply from His wisdom and knowledge.
