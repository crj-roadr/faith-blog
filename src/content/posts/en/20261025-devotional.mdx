# Devotional 2 Timothy 1: Fan into Flame the Gift of God Without Shame or Fear

Today's devotional is found in the book of 2 Timothy, chapter 1, and this is what happened.

**Synopsis:** In this chapter, Paul writes in his second letter addressed to his son in the faith, Timothy, exhorting him to fan into flame the gift of God which he received when Paul laid hands on him. Paul also encourages him to live his faith without timidity or shame. Furthermore, because of the power he received from God, he can endure troubles and persecutions for believing in Jesus Christ as our Lord and Savior.

## Observations

1. **Called to fan our faith in God into flame without timidity or shame.** This observation is very important in general, but it speaks to me personally as well. For most of my life, I think I was ashamed to admit to people that I was a Christian. I spent nearly 30 years of my life not living by the Spirit with boldness and conviction, because I was still living to please the world. I didn't want people to know I was a Christian because I feared they would judge me for believing in someone I had never seen, or blame me for the hypocrisy of many Christians, and so on. Because of these thoughts and fears, I ended up slowly drifting away from the path of God that my parents had always taught me. When someone begins to satisfy the desires of the flesh rather than the desires of the Spirit, they end up wanting to keep satisfying carnal desires and rejecting spiritual desires. This happens because spiritual and carnal desires are opposed to each other and both want the exact opposite. That is, the two cannot coexist (Galatians 5:16–17). But, thank God, through the gift of faith I received from the Holy Spirit, I never stopped believing in Him, even when I strayed. And also, thanks to my sincere search for truth, which ultimately led me back to Jesus. Because in the end, Jesus is the way, the truth, and the life, and no one comes to God the Father except through Him (John 14:6). Now, with greater wisdom and knowledge of the Word of God, I have the foundation to defend my faith in Him and the conviction and boldness to spread the gospel of Christ. Every day, I choose to live for Jesus and place my life in His hands. Even when I go through tribulations, I now understand why we all go through them—a servant is never greater than his master (John 15:20). I also find peace in distress and live with good courage, for Jesus promised and fulfilled His promise by overcoming the world and triumphing over death (John 16:33). Therefore, we must fan our faith in Jesus into flame every day, because He will never fail to fulfill His promises.
