# Devotional Titus 2: Christian Conduct in the Home and the Church

Today's devotional is found in the book of Titus, chapter 2, and this is what happened.

**Synopsis:** In this chapter, Paul writes a letter to his son in the faith, Titus, instructing him on what he must teach each member of the body of Christ. Paul tells him that older men (church leaders) must be taught to be temperate, worthy of respect, self-controlled, and sound in faith, in love, and in endurance. Older women are to be reverent in their behavior, teaching younger women to love their husbands and children, to be self-controlled and pure, working at home, kind, and submissive to their own husbands, so that no one will malign the word of God. Similarly, young men are to be encouraged to be self-controlled, setting an example by doing what is good so that no one can say anything bad about them (Christians).

## Observations

1. **What we must teach and learn regarding our conduct as members of the body of Christ.** In this chapter, Paul explains to Titus how each member of the body of Christ ought to behave according to their age and role. Unfortunately, these godly characteristics are increasingly being lost over time. Older members of the church are abdicating their responsibility to teach and mentor, while younger generations are disobeying the elders in churches and within their own families. This also occurs due to worldly influences that encourage people to be self-absorbed and to pursue earthly pleasures instead of heavenly treasures. Both Jesus Christ and Paul warned us that this degradation would happen (Matthew 24:6–7; 2 Timothy 3:1–5). To remedy this situation, we must return to faithfully following God's commandments and teachings. First, we must genuinely accept Jesus Christ as our Lord and Savior so that the Holy Spirit of God can transform our hearts (John 3:16, 7:37–39), replacing our hearts of stone with hearts of flesh. Only then can we walk in God's ways and keep His statutes (Ezekiel 36:26–27). Second, we must follow and respect the divine order established by God—that is, children submit to their mother, mothers submit to their husbands, husbands submit to Christ, and Christ submits to God (1 Corinthians 11:3; Ephesians 5:22–24; Ephesians 6:1–2). These are the first two essential steps to overcome this decline and begin truly living our lives in accordance with God's will.
