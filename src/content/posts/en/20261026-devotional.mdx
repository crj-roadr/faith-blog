# Devotional 2 Timothy 2: How to Be a Worker Approved by God

Today's devotional is found in the book of 2 Timothy, chapter 2, and this is what happened.

**Synopsis:** In this chapter, Paul writes in his second letter addressed to his son in the faith, Timothy, calling him to be a worker approved by God who treats others with love, kindness, and patience instead of engaging in pointless arguments, quarrels, and disputes that only serve to lead people further away from godliness.

## Observations

1. **We must be workers approved by God.** This is the approval we ought to seek, not the approval of the world or of ourselves. For when we surrender our lives into the hands of Jesus Christ, our lives no longer belong to us. We stop living to gain the favor of others and satisfy our carnal desires, and we begin living to satisfy the desires of the Spirit. This means that we do not seek useless arguments or quarrels that bring indignation and confuse people regarding faith in Christ (2 Timothy 2:14). There is no need to dispute over matters such as circumcision, the Jewish dietary laws, the age of the universe, and others that have nothing to do with the gospel of Christ. Such disputes merely serve as a stumbling block to others, and this is something we must never cause, because Jesus already warned us of the consequences (Luke 17:1–2). As faithful workers of God, we must strive to present ourselves as workers approved by God, of blameless character and rightly handling the Word of truth (2 Timothy 2:15). In other words, we must live to satisfy the desires of the Spirit, for against such things there is no law because they are blameless. That is, we must lead a life grounded in the following virtues—love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control (Galatians 5:22–23). The more we cultivate these fruits of the Spirit, the more we desire to satisfy the desires of the Spirit and reject carnal desires. Therefore, we must continue nourishing the desires of the Spirit, as they will help us forsake carnal desires. Only then will we be worthy of being workers approved by God.
